## Research Areas

1. **Neural data methods and mechanisms**: Algorithms, models, and theoretical
   frameworks for interpreting biological neural activity during cognition,
   with emphasis on mechanism discovery and method-driven analysis.
2. **Neuroscience of higher human cognition**: Language, mathematics, physical
   intuition, and related higher cognition, studied through psychophysics,
   cognitive neuroscience experiments, neuron-resolution human neural data,
   signal processing, and interpretation.
3. **Advanced mathematical methods for data science**: Topological data
   analysis, information geometry, functional analysis, dynamical systems, and
   related mathematical structures applied to data science and neural data
   analysis.
4. **Mathematical foundations of machine learning**: Principled mathematical
   understanding of generative models, optimization theory, learning dynamics,
   and other core ML problems, beyond phenomenological descriptions.

## Core Scientific Questions

- How does biological neural activity implement higher cognition?
- What latent structures and dynamics organize neural population activity?
- What are the neural bases of language, mathematics, and physical intuition?

## Intellectual Anchors

Relevant intellectual traditions include neural dynamics and recurrent-network
theory (Ostojic, Sussillo, Fusi), probabilistic neural-data models (Sahani,
Linderman), human higher cognition (Dehaene, Sablé-Meyer), computational cognitive
science (Tenenbaum, Gershman), and systems neuroscience experiments (Liping Wang).
