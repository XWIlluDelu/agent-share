# Returned report: Uncertainty and information

This is an authored evaluation fixture, not an original research paper. It is

For a discrete random variable, entropy H(X) is the probability-weighted average
of -log p(x). With base-two logarithms its units are bits. Entropy concerns an
assumed probability distribution; it is not a direct measure of semantic meaning.
Mutual information describes the reduction in uncertainty about one variable
associated with observing another. Independent variables have zero mutual
information under their joint distribution.

Conditional entropy and mutual information connect questions about uncertainty,
prediction, and communication. Empirical estimates depend on sampling and model
assumptions. A good explanation specifies the random variables, distribution,
logarithm base, and relevant conditioning information.
